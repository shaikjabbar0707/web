🇮🇳 Indian Culture & Heritage

Explore the beautiful traditions, arts, festivals, clothing, crafts, dances and cuisines of India.

💃 Classical🕺 Folk🪔 Festivals🎨 Arts🧵 Handicrafts👗 Clothing🍛 Cuisines

🌏 Discover the Diversity of India

India has one of the world's richest and most diverse cultural traditions. Explore different regional dances, festivals, traditional arts, handicrafts, clothing and cuisines.

💃 1. Classical Dances

Classical dance traditions representing different regions and artistic styles of India.

Bharatanatyam

📍 Tamil Nadu

Classical dance known for precise movements, hand gestures and expressive storytelling.

Kathak

📍 North India

Combines rhythmic footwork, spins, gestures and storytelling.

Kathakali

📍 Kerala

Dance-drama famous for elaborate costumes, makeup and dramatic expressions.

Kuchipudi

📍 Andhra Pradesh

Classical dance combining graceful movements, expressions and dramatic storytelling.

Odissi

📍 Odisha

Known for graceful poses, sculpturesque movements and devotional themes.

Manipuri

📍 Manipur

Characterized by gentle movements and strong connections with Vaishnava traditions.

Mohiniyattam

📍 Kerala

Graceful classical dance traditionally associated with feminine expression.

Sattriya

📍 Assam

Classical dance originating from the Vaishnavite monastic tradition of Assam.

🕺 2. Folk Dances

Traditional folk dances connected with regional communities, celebrations and traditions.

Bhangra

📍 Punjab

Energetic Punjabi folk dance traditionally associated with harvest celebrations.

Garba

📍 Gujarat

Circular group dance commonly performed during Navratri celebrations.

Ghoomar

📍 Rajasthan

Traditional Rajasthani dance recognized for graceful circular movements and colorful attire.

Bihu

📍 Assam

Traditional Assamese dance associated with Bihu celebrations and agricultural traditions.

Lavani

📍 Maharashtra

Traditional Maharashtrian performance combining rhythmic dance and expressive singing.

Yakshagana

📍 Karnataka

Traditional theatre and dance form known for elaborate costumes, music and storytelling.

Cheraw

📍 Mizoram

Traditional bamboo dance performed using coordinated rhythmic movements.

🪔 3. Festivals

Major festivals celebrated across India's diverse communities.

Diwali

📍 Pan-India

Festival of lights celebrated with lamps, decorations, family gatherings and traditional customs.

Holi

📍 Pan-India

Festival associated with colors, springtime celebrations and community gatherings.

Dussehra

📍 Pan-India

Festival marking different regional traditions associated with the victory of good over evil.

Pongal

📍 Tamil Nadu

Harvest festival celebrated with traditional foods, decorations and cultural activities.

Onam

📍 Kerala

Major Kerala festival associated with harvest traditions, feasts, flowers and cultural performances.

Eid

📍 Pan-India

Islamic festivals marked by prayer, family gatherings, charity and traditional food.

Christmas

📍 Pan-India

Christian festival celebrated through worship, decorations, family gatherings and community traditions.

Bihu

📍 Assam

Group of Assamese festivals closely connected with agricultural seasons and community traditions.

Durga Puja

📍 West Bengal

Major festival featuring artistic Durga idols, rituals, cultural events and community celebrations.

Ganesh Chaturthi

📍 Maharashtra & other regions

Festival celebrating Ganesha through worship, decorations, music and community events.

🎨 4. Traditional Arts

Traditional painting and artistic traditions of India.

Madhubani

📍 Bihar

Folk painting traditionally associated with Mithila, using distinctive patterns and vibrant imagery.

Warli

📍 Maharashtra

Tribal painting style known for simple geometric human and nature figures.

Pattachitra

📍 Odisha / West Bengal

Traditional painting on cloth or prepared surfaces featuring detailed decorative imagery.

Kalamkari

📍 Andhra Pradesh / Telangana

Textile art involving hand-drawn or block-printed traditional designs.

Gond Art

📍 Central India

Indigenous art traditionally featuring animals, nature and patterned forms.

Tanjore Painting

📍 Tamil Nadu

Decorative paintings recognized for rich ornamentation and raised or gilded elements.

🧵 5. Handicrafts

Traditional crafts created by skilled Indian artisans.

Channapatna Toys

📍 Karnataka

Traditional wooden toys known for colorful designs and craftsmanship.

Kondapalli Toys

📍 Andhra Pradesh

Lightweight wooden toys traditionally made by artisans around Kondapalli.

Dhokra

📍 Central / Eastern India

Traditional metal craft made using the lost-wax casting technique.

Bamboo Crafts

📍 Northeast India

Bamboo is used to create baskets, furniture, decorative objects and everyday products.

Pottery

📍 Pan-India

Traditional ceramic craft used for household, decorative and cultural purposes.

Textile Crafts

📍 Pan-India

Includes weaving, embroidery, printing and other regional textile traditions.

👗 6. Traditional Clothing

Traditional garments representing India's regional diversity.

Saree

📍 Pan-India

Draped garment with many regional weaving, embroidery and draping traditions.

Dhoti

📍 Various regions

Traditional wrapped garment worn in several parts of India.

Kurta

📍 Pan-India

Long tunic-style garment commonly worn in different Indian communities.

Sherwani

📍 North India

Formal long coat-style garment often worn for ceremonies and celebrations.

Mundu

📍 Kerala

Traditional wrapped garment commonly worn in Kerala.

Mekhela Chador

📍 Assam

Traditional two-piece women's attire of Assam.

Phiran

📍 Kashmir

Traditional loose garment associated with Kashmiri clothing traditions.

Lehenga

📍 Various regions

Skirt-based traditional outfit commonly worn during celebrations and ceremonies.

🍛 7. Indian Cuisines

Explore diverse food traditions from different regions of India.

South Indian

📍 South India

Includes dishes such as dosa, idli, sambar and regional rice preparations.

North Indian

📍 North India

Includes diverse breads, curries, rice dishes and regional preparations.

Bengali

📍 West Bengal

Known for rice, fish preparations, sweets and distinctive spice combinations.

Punjabi

📍 Punjab

Includes dishes such as sarson-based preparations, breads and various legumes.

Rajasthani

📍 Rajasthan

Known for dishes adapted to the region's traditional ingredients and climate.

Gujarati

📍 Gujarat

Includes a wide range of vegetarian dishes, snacks and sweets.

Kashmiri

📍 Kashmir

Includes distinctive meat, rice and vegetarian preparations influenced by Kashmiri traditions.

Hyderabadi

📍 Telangana

Famous for dishes such as biryani and a mixture of regional culinary influences.

Goan

📍 Goa

Coastal cuisine featuring seafood and distinctive regional flavors.
